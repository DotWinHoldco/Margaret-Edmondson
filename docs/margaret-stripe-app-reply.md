# Reply draft: taking payments at the holiday market

Hi Margaret — yes, you can use the official Stripe Dashboard app on your phone and sign in to your existing Art By ME Stripe account. You do not need a second Stripe account.

1. Download Stripe Dashboard from the App Store or Google Play and sign in to Art By ME. Complete its setup before the market. Tap to Pay needs a compatible phone, internet access and location permission; on iPhone it requires an iPhone XS or newer with a supported iOS version, a passcode and iCloud sign-in.
2. To make a sale, tap **+**, choose **Charge a card or send an invoice**, and enter the final amount to charge.
3. Choose **Tap to Pay**, then follow the screen while your customer taps a contactless card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Wait for the successful-payment confirmation before completing the sale.
4. Open the payment to send an email receipt. Keep a record of the artwork sold, and mark an original sold on the website separately: a payment entered directly in the Stripe app does not automatically create an Art By ME website order or update its inventory.

Stripe’s published standard US pricing for domestic-card in-person payments is 2.7% + 5¢, plus 10¢ per Tap to Pay authorization. A successful $100 sale with one authorization would therefore cost about $2.85. Your account’s agreed pricing and the payment method can differ; manually entering card details uses different pricing.

Please do not assume downloading the app turns on Texas sales-tax collection. We still need to confirm your registration and the appropriate tax setup. For an amount you type into the app, check the tax handling and final amount before charging. Zelle or Venmo is not a substitute for resolving sales tax.
