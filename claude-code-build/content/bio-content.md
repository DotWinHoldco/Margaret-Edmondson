## Section 1 — Origin / "Growing Up"

> **Heading suggestion:** "A small-town start" · **Tone:** Warm, personal, sets up the through-line of *staying* vs. *moving*.

I grew up in a small town in Southern Illinois where family-owned businesses were common and generations of families knew each other. When I was little my parents mentioned the possibility of moving houses in the same town, which would require me to change elementary schools and meet new people. I remember this clearly as I said … "absolutely not, that I would not be happy with that at all and to not do it." So they didn't! The only move that I experienced growing up was one household move in the same area of town!

## Section 2 — The Journey / "Following Family"

> **Heading suggestion:** "Ten moves in thirty years" · **Tone:** Resilience and adaptability — supports the *embraces change* narrative.

Fast forward several years since then and life has given me many unexpected adventures as I have moved out of state 10 times in 30 years! After I graduated with a BS in Art Education and a minor in music from Murray State University, Murray, Kentucky, I married my husband, Shawn, whom I met my freshman year. I followed my husband's career across the country, and embraced change! I worked as an interior designer in Southeast Missouri for 3 years until we relocated to Jacksonville, Florida. There I got to realize my dream of attending graduate school at Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD), in Savannah, Georgia. I commuted weekly to Georgia to complete my MFA in Painting. During that time my body of work became more realistic and focused on a sense of place, instability, and uncertainty that was caused by relocating far away from family and friends. Upon graduating from SCAD, I returned to teaching art to all ages in multiple states as my husband's job relocated us from Florida to Tennessee, East Texas, Northern California, North Texas, St. Louis, MO, and then back to the Dallas-Fort Worth area again. During these relocations, I focused on settling my family and I always found my way back to teaching and creating art. Now, my husband and I are celebrating 26 years of marriage and have two beautiful children who are now in high school. My son and daughter are the center of my world, but are more independent now and I find myself with more time to create.

## Section 3 — Artistic Voice / "What's in the work"

> **Heading suggestion:** "What you'll find in my work" · **Tone:** Statement of artistic vision — anchors the buyer's emotional connection to the work.

What you will find in my artwork is the beauty of what I see around me. I have consistently favored drawing and painting to express realism. I find it fascinating to document my surroundings and render what catches my eye. I often use my camera as my initial sketch and from those photographs I pair down what I see, move things around, or combine several scenes or ideas into one final piece. I am old-fashioned and do all of this by hand, preferring to sketch my ideas out on paper. I love to be inspired by my travels and the environment around me.

## Section 4 — Subjects / "What inspires"

> **Heading suggestion:** "Cattle, cactuses, coastlines" · **Tone:** Visual richness — should sit next to imagery from each series mentioned (Texas cattle, Arizona cactus, beach scenes).

In Texas, I see cattle, farm animals, and wild sunflowers that grow in fields and even on the side of highway construction zones. I love to photograph and paint my dogs. I am obsessed with cactuses and the beautiful colors that I witnessed on two short side trips to Arizona. The color palette, the landscape change with blue-violet mountains along the horizon lines that followed the perimeter in all directions, the sage green of the fascinating saguaro cactuses captivated me. The beach scenes are from family vacations to Alabama and California and I find the changing colors, space and scale breathtaking.

## Section 5 — Recent Direction / "Where I'm headed"

> **Heading suggestion:** "Experimenting with texture, text, and thread" · **Tone:** Forward-looking — links directly to the Encouragement Series and supports buyer interest in newer work.

Recently, I have been experimenting with textures, printmaking, text, and even sewing to incorporate into my work. I have enjoyed exploring mixed media collages as these explorations have contributed to awakening my curiosity, enjoyment and play of making art. This has started to chisel away at some of my perfectionist tendencies which has allowed room for chance and surprises in my work. I hope you enjoy some of the beauty that I have found in the everyday and will follow my journey through an artist's eyes.
